Air pollution caused by vehicles is one of the major environmental problems in modern cities. Harmful gases such as Carbon Monoxide (CO), Liquefied Petroleum Gas (LPG), Methane (CH₄), smoke, and toxic pollutants released from vehicles affect human health and contribute to global warming.
This project, Air Pollution Control in Vehicles, is designed to monitor harmful gas emissions from vehicles using multiple gas sensors connected to an ESP32 microcontroller. When gas concentration exceeds a predefined threshold, the system displays the gas values on an LCD and sends an SMS alert using a GSM module.
The system helps in:
Monitoring vehicle exhaust pollution
Detecting harmful gases
Providing real-time alerts
Supporting environmental protection initiatives
